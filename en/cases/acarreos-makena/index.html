Moving, hauling and freight · Panama

Acarreos Makena: from scattered WhatsApp chats to a connected business.

See the live website
acarreosmakena.com

The challenge

  • Customers only came in through WhatsApp, and quotes were scattered across chats.
  • There was no website showing up on Google when people searched for their services.
  • There was no way to show customer reviews or measure how many quote requests came in.

What we did

  1. 01

    Website on its own domain

    Custom design for the brand, fast on mobile, with a direct WhatsApp button and a quote form.

  2. 02

    SEO with service and location pages

    Pages for moving, hauling, debris removal, event and office setup, plus locations like San Miguelito, Panamá Oeste and Chiriquí. Sitemap and Search Console set up.

  3. 03

    CRM on Google Sheets

    Every quote request from the form is saved to a sheet with its status: new, contacted, quoted, scheduled, won or lost. With an automatic summary.

  4. 04

    Reviews with approval

    Customers rate with stars and leave a comment; the owner decides which ones get published.

What's up and running

  • Website live at acarreosmakena.com
  • 10+ pages built for Google searches
  • Quote form connected to the CRM and WhatsApp
  • Star reviews with owner approval
  • Sitemap submitted to Google Search Console
