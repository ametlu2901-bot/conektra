FAQ

What people ask us most.

If your question isn't here, message us on WhatsApp or email hola@enlazastudio.com.

What exactly does Enlaza Studio do?

We connect the parts of your business that work separately today: your website, WhatsApp, customers, payments and reports. You can start with one solution and add the rest later.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. After the diagnosis we send you a proposal with a clear price before we start. You can also work with a monthly plan.

How do automatic payment reminders work?

We record what you're owed and when it's due. The system sends WhatsApp reminders before and after the due date, and you see in one list who owes, how much and since when.

Can you connect DGI e-invoicing or Yappy?

It's evaluated per project. Before promising it we check what each service allows and test it with you. That's why we label them «needs development».

Do I have to change the tools I use?

Rarely. We often connect what you already have, like WhatsApp and Google Sheets. If something can't do more, we tell you and suggest an alternative.

Do I need to know about technology?

No. We explain everything in plain words and leave the system working and documented.

Is my data safe?

Your data stays in your own accounts. We use minimal-permission access and hand everything over documented.

Do you guarantee first place on Google?

No one can honestly guarantee that. We leave your website technically ready and built around what your customers search for.

What happens after delivery?

We stay with you for support and improvements. If you add a service, a branch or a new tool, we connect it.
