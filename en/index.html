Services

What exactly does Conektra do?

We build websites, systems to organize customers and quotes (CRM) and connections between your tools. You can start with just one and add the rest later.

I already have WhatsApp and Instagram. Why a website?

Because when someone searches "movers in Panama" or "AC technician" on Google, they don't see your Instagram: they see websites. Your website puts you in those searches and sends that person straight to your WhatsApp.

Pricing and hiring

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need: how many services and areas to show and which features it includes. We send you a proposal with a clear price before we start.

Do I have to hire everything at once?

No. Start with what you need today, like your website, and add more when your business calls for it.

Development

How long does it take?

It depends on the number of pages and features. We give you the exact timeline in the proposal, before we start.

Do I need to know about technology?

No. We explain everything in plain words, and your quotes land in a Google Sheets spreadsheet you use like any table.

Google and SEO

Do you guarantee first place on Google?

No one can honestly guarantee that. What we do is leave your website technically ready, with pages built for what your customers search for.

What is local SEO?

It's preparing your website to show up when someone searches for your service in your area, for example "pest control in San Miguelito".

CRM and automation

Do I need to pay for a CRM license?

Not necessarily. Depending on your case we can use tools you already have, like Google Sheets, or connect an off-the-shelf CRM.

How do reviews work?

Your customers rate you with stars from your website and you decide which ones get published.

Integrations

Can you connect Yappy or other payment gateways?

It can be evaluated per project. Before promising it, we check what each service allows and test it with you.

Do I have to replace the tools I already use?

Not necessarily. We often connect what you already have. If something can't keep up, we'll tell you and suggest an alternative.

Support

What happens after delivery?

We stay with you for support and improvements. If you add a service, an area or a new tool, we add it.

Data ownership

Is my data safe?

Your data stays in your own accounts, like your Google Sheets. We use least-privilege access and hand everything over documented.
