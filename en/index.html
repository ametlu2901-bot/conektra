FAQ

What people ask us most.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on which systems need to be connected and how much needs to be built. Tell us about your case and we'll send a proposal with clear scope and price before we start.

Do I have to replace the systems I already use?

Not necessarily. We often connect what you already have. If something can't keep up, we'll tell you and suggest an alternative.

Do you work with businesses outside Panama?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses in Panama and abroad, in English or Spanish.

What happens after delivery?

We stay with you for support and improvements. Your systems grow with you, so we can always add new integrations.

Is my data safe?

Your data stays in your own accounts and systems. We use least-privilege access and hand everything over documented.
