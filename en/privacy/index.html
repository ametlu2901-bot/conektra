Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What we collect

Only what you send us through the contact form: your name, your business name, your WhatsApp, your email if you choose to share it, what you need, your approximate budget if you give one and your message. If you message us on WhatsApp, also your number and what you tell us.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, prepare a proposal and follow up on your request. We don't sell it or share it with third parties for advertising.

Where it's stored

Form messages are stored in a private Google spreadsheet that only Conektra can access.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts; these services may log technical visit data, such as your IP address, under their own policies. We don't use advertising cookies or tracking tools.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we have about you, to correct it or to delete it. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll take care of it.
