How long does a website take?

It depends on the number of pages and features. A simple site can be ready in a few weeks; we give you the exact timeline in the proposal.

Can I edit it myself later?

Yes. We show you how to make basic changes, and we can also handle them for you.

Do you guarantee first place on Google?

No one can honestly guarantee that. What we do is leave your site technically ready, with content built for your customers' searches.
