FAQ

Custom CRM

Do I need to pay for a CRM license?

Not necessarily. Depending on your case we can use Google Sheets or connect an off-the-shelf CRM.

Can my team use it without long training?

That's the idea: we design it with your stages and your words, and support you at the start.

Does it connect to my website?

Yes. Your website form can send every request straight to the CRM.
